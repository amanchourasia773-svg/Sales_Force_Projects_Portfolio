# Orient Electric Limited (OEL) – Sales & Customer Service Application

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on the implementation, configuration, and maintenance of Salesforce CRM solutions for **Orient Electric Limited (OEL)**, a division of the CK Birla Group. The project supports Small and Medium Business (SMB) sales operations and customer service processes within the retail and supply chain management domain.

The solution leverages **Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud** to improve sales activity management, customer relationship management, service operations, and release management.

## 📖 Project Description

Orient Electric Limited is a manufacturer and marketer of lifestyle electrical products, including:

* Fans
* Lighting Solutions
* Home Appliances
* Switchgear Products

The project involved supporting the digitization and optimization of SMB sales operations and customer service management using Salesforce CRM.

The implementation focused on Salesforce configuration, security administration, data management, release coordination, testing, deployment, and ongoing production support.
